# Candidate profile (example)

The skill copies this file to `$home/input/profile.md` if that path is missing and no LinkedIn PDF import ran. Replace every placeholder. Phase 0 may rewrite the copy from `$home/input/profile.pdf`. Phase 1 and 2 hunt from the copy, not from the skill prose.

## Role target

Your role and level (for example Product Manager, Staff Engineer, Designer). List titles you want and titles to reject.

## Core stack

Your tools and skills. Comma-separated. Leave empty only if the CV should supply them.

## Focus

What kind of work you want (domain, product area, or craft). This is the Focus fit axis.

## Location

Where you can work (remote-only, specific regions, hybrid, on-site). Spell out relocation rules.

## Hard filters

Roles or topics to drop. Add only what you mean. An empty list means no extra excludes.

## Company signal

Companies, products, or kinds of orgs you want. Replace with your list.
